JLab Audio Analytics Project

Customer Retention & Data-Driven Marketing Strategy for Audio Technology Company

Role

Data Analyst & Marketing Strategist

Duration

7 months

Company

JLab Audio (San Diego)

Focus Area

Customer Analytics & Retention Strategy

Overview

One-time buyers are one of the biggest revenue leaks in e-commerce, and JLab, a San Diego-based audio brand, was no exception. As part of a team engaged directly with JLab, I worked on analyzing their customer behavior data and developing a data-driven strategy to convert one-time and in-person purchasers into loyal, repeat online customers.

What I Built

Working alongside my team, I helped consolidate and clean purchasing data across four platforms — Shopify, TikTok, in-person retail, and JLab's website — and contributed to building a full customer analytics pipeline. This included RFM modeling, cohort analysis, customer segmentation, and behavioral analysis across seasonal trends, regional preferences, and cross-channel conversion rates. Together, we identified 5 distinct customer segments with meaningfully different lifetime values and purchase behaviors.

Results

The analysis surfaced several high-impact opportunities: untapped seasonal revenue windows beyond traditional holidays, region-specific product preferences, and strong purchase affinity patterns ideal for bundling. On the marketing side, our redesigned Klaviyo email flows — built using our segmentation data.

Impact & Next Steps

This engagement delivered actionable strategies JLab could implement immediately — personalized product recommendations, data-driven bundles, and optimized retention flows. It demonstrated how multi-platform behavioral data, when properly unified, can transform a fragmented customer base into a targetable, retention-ready audience. Going forward, I'd look to layer in predictive churn modeling to proactively identify at-risk customers before they lapse.
