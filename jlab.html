JLab Audio Analytics Project

Customer Retention & Data-Driven Marketing Strategy for Audio Technology Company

Overview

As part of a customer analytics project with JLab, a San Diego-based audio brand, I worked with my team to understand customer purchasing behavior and develop strategies to increase repeat purchases, particularly among one-time and in-store buyers.

What I Did

We consolidated and cleaned purchasing data from Shopify, TikTok, physical retail, and JLab's website. Using RFM modeling and cohort analysis, we identified five customer segments based on purchase frequency, spending, and customer value. We also analyzed seasonal trends, regional product preferences, and purchasing patterns across sales channels.

Results

Our analysis identified sales opportunities beyond major holidays, regional differences in product demand, and products frequently purchased together. Based on these findings, we recommended targeted product bundles, personalized recommendations, and redesigned Klaviyo email flows tailored to different customer segments.

Impact & Next Steps

We presented JLab with a customer segmentation framework and practical recommendations to improve repeat purchases and email marketing. As a next step, I would explore predictive churn modeling to identify customers likely to stop purchasing and target them with retention campaigns.
