Cancer Screening Reminder System

Project Overview

Working with three teammates, I helped develop a Cancer Screening Reminder System designed to provide personalized screening recommendations based on age and individual risk factors. The project focused on making screening information more accessible, particularly for underserved communities.

What We Did

Our team analyzed California Cancer Registry data to identify cancer incidence patterns across age, sex, and ethnicity. We used U.S. Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) guidelines to develop screening recommendation logic based on user information.

We also created the foundation of an interactive interface that displays age-specific screening reminders and helps users understand which screenings may be relevant to them.

Results

We developed a prototype that combines public health data with established screening guidelines to provide personalized reminders. The project demonstrates how data and technology can help make cancer screening information easier to access and understand.
